      Missable trophies

      There is one potentially missable trophy in GTA VI. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story, because there is no mission replay — so a missed meditation cannot be redone without an earlier save. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

        Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap and GTABase, both flagging Positive Mental Attitude as the only potentially missable trophy.
